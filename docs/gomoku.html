Gomoku-NUSV

A standalone, cross-platform Gomoku (Five in a Row) game for macOS, Windows, iOS and Android — with on-device AI opponents and zero network dependencies.

01 Overview

Gomoku-NUSV is a board game built with Kotlin Multiplatform + Compose Multiplatform, shipping as a native app on four platforms from one codebase. Play against a friend on the same screen, or challenge the built-in AI across three difficulty levels.

The game started as a minigame inside NUSV LITE and grew into its own standalone project — a small showcase of how far the NUSV stack can go on desktop and mobile.

02 The problem it solves

  • Board games without baggage — no account, no ads, no network connection, no cloud AI. The entire game engine runs on your device, so it works on a plane, in a blackout, or anywhere else.
  • Same code, four platforms — one Kotlin Multiplatform codebase covers macOS, Windows, iOS and Android, which keeps the project maintainable for a small team (or one person).
  • Serious play on mobile and desktop — most Gomoku apps are either too simple or mobile-only; this one brings a deep board experience with adjustable AI strength to the desktop too.

03 Highlights

Play

Local two-playerTwo humans, one screen.
Three AI levelsEasy (heuristics), Medium (threat search, 4 plies) and Hard (threat search, up to 8 plies on desktop).
Device-aware AISearch depth and time budget adapt to the device — and truncate gracefully when time runs out.
Optional move timer10–300 second countdown; timeout forfeits the game.
Undo / resign / restartFull game control, including undo of both sides' moves against the AI.
PersistenceAn unfinished game is saved automatically and offers to resume on next launch.

Depth & feel

  • Board sizes 13×13, 15×15 and 19×19, with ten fully unlocked color themes (wood, deep space, ocean, bamboo, mint, rose, violet, maple, midnight, ink).
  • Board rendering — gradient stones with highlights and shadows, drop-in animation for the last move, hover preview and a last-move marker.
  • Sci-fi effects — energy ripples on moves, particle bursts, a hologram scan beam and a neon winning-line trail.
  • Synthesized sound — place, win, draw and timeout sounds are generated on-device; no audio assets.
  • Statistics — persistent profile tracking wins, losses, draws, streaks and per-difficulty performance.
  • Power-ups — an AI hint and +30s time boosts, consumed from your stock (new players start with some).
