01 Overview

NUSV LITE is the official Android client for the NUSV ecosystem, built in Kotlin with Jetpack Compose. It is one home screen for everything NUSV: browse the latest content across NUSV sites organized by category, kill time with 11 built-in games, get real things done with 60+ tools, and personalize the experience with widgets and themes.

The app is also the entry point to the points system — daily check-ins and game wins earn points, tying the ecosystem together with a light, playful economy.

02 The problem it solves

NUSV's content and services are spread across multiple sites and formats, with no single lightweight entry point. A dedicated app solves that in one stroke:

  • One hub instead of many tabs — content from every NUSV site is aggregated into one categorized feed, readable offline.
  • An offline companion — games and tools work with no network at all; content updates are pulled as versioned sync files (see nusv-lite-sync), so the app only downloads what changed.
  • Engagement with a reason — the check-in and points system gives everyday visits a small reward loop.

03 Highlights

Content & customization

Content hubLatest posts from all NUSV sites, organized by category with color-coded sorting.
Versioned syncContent and docs update over the network as versioned JSON — cheap, incremental and offline-cacheable.
WidgetsHome-screen widgets bring NUSV content and shortcuts to your launcher.
Theme storePersonalize the whole app — themes are first-class citizens.

Games

Eleven built-in games — Tic-Tac-Toe, 2048, Minesweeper, Memory Match, Snake, Wordle, Simon Says, Whack-a-Mole, Tetris, Gomoku (against a threat-aware AI with two difficulty levels) and Sudoku. Wins earn points and per-game high scores are tracked.

Tools

More than 60 utilities in three categories:

  • Everyday: dice & coin flips, calculators (tip / BMI / age), unit & currency converters, world clock, stopwatch, countdown, pomodoro, bill splitter, expense tracker, flashlight, battery info, lottery generator.
  • Dev: JSON formatter, Base64, hash, UUID, epoch / URL / color / case converters, regex tester, Markdown preview, QR generator, text statistics, password generator, text encryption.
  • Creative: drawing pad, kaomoji keyboard, name generator, random quotes, biorhythm charts, Morse code, metronome, breathing exercise.

Polish

  • Game sound effects with haptics, and remote sync for content — details that make a pocket app feel alive.
  • MIT-licensed and dependency-light, easy to build from source with the included scripts.
